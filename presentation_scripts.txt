Speaker Script:"One of the greatest challenges in space medicine is avoiding false alarms caused by normal zero-gravity adaptation. On Earth, mild hypotension, polycythemia, or a leaky heart valve can be concerning. But in orbit, fluid shifts normalize hypotension, and a higher red blood cell baseline acts as a natural buffer against space anemia. Astra incorporates Adaptive Baseline Intelligence, automatically recalibrating thresholds so it never triggers false panics for normal physiological shifts.   However, when a true environmental hazard arises, Astra acts instantly. In Scenario 01, a scrubber lag causes cabin to drift, pushing the crew's respiration to 24 breaths per minute and dropping blood oxygen to 93%. Instead of treating individual illness, Astra cross-references multi-crew vests and module locations, isolates the environmental root cause, automatically ramps up module filtration fans, and vibrates the crew's shirts to guide paced breathing!"
